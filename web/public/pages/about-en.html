Information

A new software solution connected to digital libraries, operated in the Kramerius system, enables searching, selection, and subsequent export of digital data (image data, OCR, metadata) either in its original form or in one of several formats that also allow efficient machine processing. The DL4DH Feeder offers enriched data and metadata thanks to additional software components developed within the DL4DH project, including Kramerius+ and the TEI converter. The DL4DH Feeder is primarily designed for humanities-oriented researchers, providing them the ability to extract data from digital libraries and utilize computational methods for research classified under digital humanities.

The project 'DL4DH - Development of Tools for More Effective Use and Extraction of Data from Digital Libraries to Strengthen Research in Digital Humanities' was supported by the Ministry of Culture of the Czech Republic within the NAKI II applied research program under ID DG20P02OVV002 and was carried out in the years 2020 - 2022.
